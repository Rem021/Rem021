## Camera research · 相机研究

**[Sony FX3 / FX6 Lab](https://github.com/Rem021/sony-fx3-fx6-lab)** — Firmware research with QEMU, browser menu tools, and offline checks. 用 QEMU、菜单工具与离线校验研究相机固件。

Research tools, not a ready-to-flash camera upgrade. Firmware-dependent experiments require your own matching official firmware. 研究工具，不是可直接刷入的相机升级包。
